# ECHO Education

**ECHO** is an educational AI concept built around one rule:

> The teacher teaches. AI stays with the student between lessons.

The project is not intended to replace teachers. A real teacher leads the lesson and owns the teaching method, materials, and educational decisions. ECHO is a clearly identified AI assistant that helps a student after class: revisiting what was unclear, practicing, and completing homework as a guided dialogue instead of copying a ready-made answer.

## First prototype

The first prototype is intentionally small:

- subject: **Мистецтво / Art**
- language: Ukrainian
- one student
- one AI assistant representing the teaching style of one teacher
- realtime voice conversation in the browser
- simple stylized avatar
- no camera
- no accounts
- no database
- no teacher dashboard
- no analytics
- no curriculum engine

The prototype exists to answer one question:

**Will a child naturally engage with a teacher-like AI assistant in a spoken after-class learning conversation?**

## Working principles

- The AI is an assistant to a real teacher, not a replacement.
- The child must always understand that the character is AI.
- Homework should favor dialogue and practical activity over generating a final answer.
- The teacher remains responsible for pedagogy and important decisions.
- We build in very small, reviewable steps.
- Codex implements only explicitly approved tasks.
